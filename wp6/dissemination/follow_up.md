# Follow-up research strategies and exploitation

This section describes how the approach developed in InProSSA will be further
developed after the end of the project. The strategy pursues two tracks: a
follow-up COMET project that transfers the approach to industry, and the
continued refinement of the method in the APA project.

## COMET project

In 2026, together with the scientific partners FH Hagenberg and Software
Competence Center Hagenberg, a COMET proposal was planned in which the developed
approach would have been transferred to industry. Due to the difficult economic
situation, industrial partners were very reluctant to commit to such a project,
so the application was postponed until 2028.

## Auto Prescriptive Analytics (APA)

At the end of the project, the approach will be further refined in the
[Auto Prescriptive Analytics](https://www.prescriptiveanalytics.at/) project
(funded by the government of Upper Austria in the program #upperVISION2030).
The goal is to overcome the current limitations and refine the approach to the
point where it can be applied in industrial research projects.
